# Scrapedge
Turn the web into knowledge. Scrapedge distills the noise of the internet, extracting structured content and transforming it into AI-powered insights.

## Getting Started
1. **Sign Up/Login**: Create an account to access your personal knowledge base.
2. **Access Dashboard**: Once authenticated, you will be directed to your dashboard where you can manage and monitor your extractions.

## Core Workflow
### 1. Input Sources
Provide the data you want to distill:
- **Single URL**: Paste a direct link to an article or page.
- **Bulk URLs**: Upload or paste multiple links for batch processing.
- **Search Query**: Enter a keyword or phrase to let Scrapedge find and extract relevant content.

### 2. Extraction & Distillation
The Firecrawl engine strips away ads, navigation menus, and clutter, leaving only pure content. This content is then passed through our AI distillation pipeline.

### 3. Review Insights
View your results in the dashboard:
- **Clean Content**: Access the raw, noise-free text.
- **AI Summaries**: Get concise, structured summaries of the extracted data.
- **Structured Output**: Export data ready for your database or documentation.

## Key Features
- **Bulk Extraction**: Process hundreds of URLs simultaneously.
- **AI Summaries**: Instant gist generation powered by state-of-the-art LLMs.
- **Intent-Based Search**: Find precise information without browsing pages of results.
- **Clean Data**: Structured output designed for seamless integration.
